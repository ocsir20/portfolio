O'town Garage Makerspace Website Design

Website design for a community makerspace—showcasing tools, membership, and events with a clear, welcoming digital presence.

Project Summary

I designed the website for O'town Garage Makerspace—a community workshop where makers access tools, training, and shared space. The work covered brand-aligned UI, responsive web and mobile layouts, wireframes through high-fidelity screens, and visual language that explains membership, facilities, and how to get involved.

🇩🇰 O'town Garage Makerspace

A makerspace offering tools, workshops, and community for builders and creators. The website needed to communicate what the space offers, build trust with new members, and make it easy to explore services on desktop and mobile.

Role
UX/UI Designer
Team
Makerspace stakeholders, marketing, and development partners.
Methods
Wireframes · High-fidelity UI · Responsive design · Design system · Brand & imagery · Mobile UX
Tools
Figma, Miro, and collaboration tools for handoff with development.

O'town Garage Makerspace needed a website that welcomes new visitors, explains what the space offers, and guides people toward membership and bookings. The design balances a practical, tool-forward tone with warmth and community.

Website design goals

The site introduces the makerspace quickly, surfaces key services and equipment, and stays clear on mobile. Wireframes through final UI aligned typography, color, imagery, and components for a consistent experience across pages.
