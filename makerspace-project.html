O'town Garage Makerspace Website Design

Website design and web development for a community makerspace. It showcases tools, membership, and events with a clear, welcoming digital presence.

Project Summary

I designed and developed the website for O'town Garage Makerspace, a community workshop where makers access tools, training, and shared space. The work covered brand-aligned UI, responsive web and mobile layouts, wireframes through high-fidelity screens, and front-end build of the live site so membership, facilities, and how to get involved are easy to understand. Outcomes include 20% more trafic on the website and 10% more bookings for workshops.

🇩🇰 O'town Garage Makerspace

The O'Town Garage is a makerspace located in Odense, Denmark. Anyone can become a member and in doing so they gain access to equipment for laser cutting, 3D printing, wood work and much more. At the makerspace, members help and exchange knowledge with each other which creates a strong community of people who share similar passions and interests.

Impact of new design

  • The new website made it simpler for people to find relevant information which resulted in no more emails and calls asking for missing information
  • Better placement in search results which led to 2x / 200% more traffic
  • Better navigation resulting in no more manual intervention to help members with their admin (member page)
  • The webpage is a professional presentation of The O'Town Garage that inspires confidence to potential sponsors and supporters
