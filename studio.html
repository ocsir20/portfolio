👋 About me

I'm Octavia Sirbu, a Designer and Developer helping small companies and freelancers across Europe turn their expertise into clean, high-converting websites.

Most small businesses get stuck choosing between templates that look generic or agencies that move too slowly. By combining both design and front-end development under one roof, I take your website from initial concept in Figma to a fast, live product without the usual bottlenecks.

My approach is backed by 7+ years of experience designing and shipping digital products in Denmark and France. I led product design and AI-ready design systems as Design Lead at Nabogo ApS, simplified complex web architectures at the University of Southern Denmark, and worked in visual marketing at Universal Robots.

Now, I bring that exact mix of commercial strategy, Scandinavian design simplicity, and modern AI-assisted development directly to independent professionals and growing European businesses.
