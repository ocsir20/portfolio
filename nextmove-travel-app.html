NextMove Travel App

Created an end-to-end trip planning journey with faster booking and cleaner travel details.

NextMove targets frequent travelers who stitch flights, stays, and ground transport in one trip. The product prioritizes quick edits, transparent pricing changes, and offline-safe access to itineraries.

Journey mapping

Key flows include inspiration search, saved trips, collaborative planning with friends, and day-of notifications. Each stage has a narrow job to be done so screens stay focused under distraction.

Visual system

Typography and iconography lean on large headlines and airy whitespace for marketing moments, then tighten density inside booking and ticket views where scanning speed matters.

Roadmap

Later releases would add group splitting for expenses, richer map layers, and partner integrations for local experiences booked from the same trip canvas.
