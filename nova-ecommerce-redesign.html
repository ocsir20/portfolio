Nova eCommerce Redesign

Optimized product discovery and checkout with clearer navigation and stronger visual hierarchy.

Nova needed a storefront that felt premium while staying fast on mid-range devices. Discovery, comparison, and checkout were treated as one continuous story instead of three disconnected templates.

Navigation and catalog

Mega menus were replaced with progressive disclosure: fewer top-level labels, smarter defaults, and filters that remember context when shoppers jump between categories.

Checkout trust and clarity

Shipping and payment steps use explicit summaries, inline validation, and a persistent order recap. Visual weight tilts toward the primary action without hiding secondary paths like guest checkout or saved cards.

Measurement

The redesign sets up experiments on search refinements, cart recovery, and payment friction. Baseline metrics should be captured before rollout to validate lift on conversion and average order value.
