HealthHub Patient Portal

Built an accessible patient experience for bookings, records, and doctor communication.

HealthHub brings appointments, documents, and messaging into one calm interface. Patients needed predictable paths, strong contrast, and language that matched how care teams speak in real life.

Research and information architecture

Interviews highlighted anxiety around rescheduling and confusion about preparation steps. We reorganized tasks by intent—book, prepare, follow up—and surfaced status on the home surface instead of burying it in nested menus.

Accessibility and UI patterns

Form patterns follow consistent error placement, live regions for async updates, and keyboard-friendly date pickers. Components reuse spacing and type scales so the portal feels coherent across mobile and desktop widths.

Next steps

The next iteration would connect analytics to completion funnels and run moderated tests on the messaging module with clinicians in the loop.
