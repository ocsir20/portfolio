How to Design Onboarding That Converts

Five practical UX patterns that improve activation and retention from day one.

Great onboarding removes uncertainty quickly. Start by reducing first session friction, then guide users to one core success moment. Use clear progress indicators, contextual tips, and value-first screens instead of long tutorials.

Track activation metrics such as first task completion, time-to-value, and drop-off by step. Iterating with these insights usually delivers faster gains than large redesigns.
