Measuring UX Impact with Metrics

Connect design decisions to product and revenue metrics.

Octavia Sirbu August 19, 2024

Tie each UX change to a specific behavior: completion rate, time on task, churn, or conversion uplift. This keeps design conversations focused on outcomes instead of opinions.

Use a baseline, run experiments when possible, and report results in plain language so stakeholders can understand business impact quickly.
