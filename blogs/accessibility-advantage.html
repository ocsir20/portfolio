Accessibility Is a Product Advantage

How inclusive design leads to better business outcomes.

Accessibility improves usability for everyone. Better contrast, keyboard support, semantic structure, and clear labels reduce errors and make experiences faster for all users.

Teams that include accessibility early also reduce rework and legal risk. Build accessibility checks into design reviews and handoff to keep quality consistent across releases.
