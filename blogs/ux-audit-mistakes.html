Common UX Audit Mistakes

What to avoid when reviewing an existing digital product.

Many audits stop at visual critique and miss behavioral evidence. Combine heuristic review with analytics, support tickets, and user sessions to uncover real friction.

Prioritize findings by impact and effort, then convert insights into a practical roadmap. Clear prioritization is what turns audits into measurable product progress.
