Privacy

This page explains what happens with your data when you use octaviasirbu.com.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who is responsible

This website is published by Octavia Sirbu, Senior Product Designer, based in Paris, France. For questions about privacy, write to octaviasirbu@gmail.com.

What this site is

octaviasirbu.com is a personal portfolio. It presents my CV, projects, and writing. It does not have user accounts, a newsletter, a shop, or a contact form that stores messages on this site.

Data I receive from you

I only receive personal data if you choose to contact me, for example by email or through LinkedIn. That typically includes your name, email address, and whatever you write. I use that information to reply and, if relevant, to continue a professional conversation. I do not sell it or use it for advertising.

I keep correspondence as long as it is useful for that conversation, then delete it when it is no longer needed, unless I have a legal reason to keep it longer.

What stays in your browser

The light and dark theme preference is saved in your browser with localStorage, under the key portfolio-theme. That stays on your device. I cannot read it. You can clear it by deleting site data in your browser settings.

This site does not set marketing cookies and does not use analytics tools such as Google Analytics, Hotjar, or Meta Pixel.

Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you load a page, GitHub may process technical data such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. That processing is done by GitHub as a service provider. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Fonts and other services

Type is loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser may request those files from Google, which can involve your IP address. If you follow a link to LinkedIn or another external site, that site has its own privacy policy.

Videos and images on this portfolio are hosted with the site. Playing a film does not send your data to a third-party video platform.

Your rights

If I hold personal data about you, you can ask to access it, correct it, delete it, restrict how it is used, or object to the use. You can also ask for a copy of data you provided. Write to octaviasirbu@gmail.com.

If you are in the EU or EEA and you think your data has not been handled correctly, you can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In France that is the CNIL.

Changes

If this notice changes, I will update the date at the top of this page.
