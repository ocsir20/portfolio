Leading Product UX for a SaaS Platform Nabogo ApS

Interactive prototype for selecting multiple laundry machines and paying in one bundled transaction.

Summary

This project explores how people choose time slots and multiple machines in shared laundry rooms, then pay once instead of juggling separate bookings. The focus was clarity under time pressure and trust at checkout.

Role
Lead Product Designer
Team
Nabogo ApS product team: 1 product manager, 1 lead product designer, 1 UX designer, 4 developers.
Methods
Sketches · User flows · High-fidelity prototyping · Error-state design · Usability testing · Design System · A/B testing

Problem and constraints

Operators needed a flow that worked on small screens, supported washer and dryer mixes, and reduced abandoned sessions when slots looked busy. We kept primary actions visible and limited cognitive load per step.

Interaction and visual decisions

Segmented controls separate washer and dryer contexts. Slot cards show availability at a glance, with steppers only where multiple machines per slot make sense. Confirmation bundles line items so the total always matches mental models.

Outcome

The prototype is ready for usability sessions with operators and end users. A standalone HTML build can be linked here when you host the interactive version alongside this case study.
